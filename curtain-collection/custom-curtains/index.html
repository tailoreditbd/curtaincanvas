The importance of a custom curtain

Why a customized curtain solution matters

Pre-stitched curtains are built to average measurements and generic taste, which means they rarely sit right once they're actually hung. A customized approach removes that guesswork at every stage. Here's what tends to go wrong without it:

  • Standard curtain widths and drops leave awkward gaps around bay, arched, or oversized windows
  • Off-the-rack fabrics may not hold up against Dhaka's heat, humidity, and dust
  • Generic pleat styles can clash with the rest of the room's design language
  • Fixed hardware options don't always suit heavier fabrics, tracks, or motorized setups
  • Light control and privacy needs differ from room to room and are rarely addressed by ready-made curtains

Four core decisions

What our custom curtain solution covers

Our custom curtain service is built around four decisions—fabric, size, style, and hardware—each shaped by your space and how you plan to use it.

01

Fabric customization

Choose from blackout, sheer, linen, velvet, and layered fabric options selected for durability, texture, and how the light moves through them.

02

Size & fit customization

Curtains are cut to your window's exact width, drop, and stack-back requirements, including bay windows, arches, and floor-to-ceiling openings.

03

Style & finishing customization

Select your pleat type, header style, lining, and trims so the finished curtain matches the room's formality and design direction.

04

Hardware customization

Pick rods, tracks, brackets, and motorized systems suited to your fabric weight, curtain style, and how you want the curtain to operate.

Who it's for

Who needs custom curtain

A custom curtain works best when a space has particular requirements that a fixed catalogue option can't fully answer.

  • Homeowners with non-standard window shapes or sizes, such as bays, arches, or corner windows
  • Clients who already have a fabric, color, or design idea they want executed precisely
  • Anyone needing specific light control or privacy performance, such as full blackout for a bedroom or media room
  • Interior designers and architects sourcing curtains for a bespoke project brief
  • Offices, hotels, and other commercial spaces with uniform window sizing across multiple rooms
