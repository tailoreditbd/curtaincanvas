Expert curtain consultancy service

The right curtain starts with understanding the room. Our curtain consultancy service helps you select fabrics, colors, styles, and fitting solutions based on your interior, lighting conditions, privacy requirements, and lifestyle.

  • Interior Assessment: We consider your room layout, furniture, wall colors, lighting, window proportions, and existing décor before recommending suitable curtain options.
  • Fabric & Color Selection: Explore fabrics, textures, patterns, and colors selected to complement your interior design.
  • Light & Privacy Planning: Choose between blackout, sheer, layered, or light-filtering curtains based on the level of sunlight and privacy your space requires.
  • Curtain Style & Hardware Guidance: Get expert advice on curtain length, pleats, rods, tracks, accessories, and motorized systems.
  • Consultation & Catalogue: Explore suitable options with professional guidance before finalizing your curtain design.

Our design philosophy

Custom-made curtains for your space

Standard curtains are made for standard windows. Your interior deserves something more considered. Curtain Canvas creates made-to-measure curtains where the dimensions, fabric, design, pleats, stitching, finishing, and fitting are planned specifically for your windows and interior. Our custom curtain process:

  1. 01
    Consultation

    We understand your interior style, practical needs, preferred design, and project requirements.

  2. 02
    Precise Measurement

    Your windows and fitting areas are measured to determine the correct curtain dimensions.

  3. 03
    Fabric & Design Selection

    Choose suitable fabrics, colors, textures, curtain styles, pleats, and finishing details.

  4. 04
    Custom Tailoring

    Your curtains are stitched and finished according to the approved measurements and design.

  5. 05
    Professional Installation

    Our team installs the curtain, rod, or track and checks the final alignment, movement, and curtain fall.
