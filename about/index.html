About Curtain Canvas: luxury curtain consultancy & custom curtains in Bangladesh

At Curtain Canvas, we believe the right curtain should do more than cover a window. It should work naturally with the architecture, furniture, lighting, colors, and character of the space around it.

We are an interior design-focused curtain brand in Bangladesh, providing thoughtfully planned curtain solutions for homes, apartments, offices, hotels, restaurants, and commercial interiors. Instead of beginning with a fabric catalogue alone, we begin by understanding the space—how much natural light enters the room, how much privacy is required, what the interior looks like, and how the curtain will be used every day.

From curtain consultancy and precise measurement to fabric selection, custom tailoring, rods and tracks, professional installation, and after-sales support, Curtain Canvas brings the entire curtain journey together under one service.

Our purpose is simple: to help every client choose curtains that look considered, perform properly, and feel like a natural part of their interior.

Why we began

Our story

Curtain Canvas was built around a simple observation: choosing a curtain can become surprisingly complicated.

A fabric may look beautiful in a showroom but feel completely different once placed beside a particular wall color, flooring style, or furniture arrangement. A curtain may provide the right appearance but fail to deliver enough privacy. Another may block sunlight effectively but make the room feel heavier than intended.

That is why we wanted Curtain Canvas to be more than a traditional curtain shop in Bangladesh.

Our approach combines curtain expertise with interior thinking. We consider proportion, texture, color, natural light, privacy, curtain fall, hardware, and functionality before recommending a solution.

Whether a client is decorating a new apartment, renovating a bedroom, furnishing a corporate office, or completing a hospitality project, our aim is to make curtain selection clearer, more personalised, and more reliable.

Where we are headed

Our vision

Our vision is to help change the way people in Bangladesh think about curtains. We want curtain planning to become a considered part of interior design—where aesthetics, comfort, privacy, lighting, and functionality are planned together from the beginning.

By combining curtain consultancy, premium materials, customization, craftsmanship, and professional installation, Curtain Canvas aims to create window solutions that make interiors feel more complete.

How we think about curtains

Our curtain philosophy

A well-designed interior is created through balance. Curtains occupy a significant visual area within most rooms, which means their color, texture, height, and volume can influence the entire atmosphere of the interior. For that reason, we approach curtains as an important part of interior planning rather than an accessory selected at the end of a project.

Our curtain philosophy focuses on four essential elements:

  • Interior Harmony. The curtain should complement your furniture, wall colors, flooring, lighting, and overall interior concept rather than compete with them.
  • Functional Comfort. Privacy, sunlight, glare, temperature, accessibility, and everyday usage are considered alongside appearance.
  • Accurate Proportion. Curtain height, width, pleat ratio, length, and placement influence how spacious and balanced a room feels.
  • Thoughtful Detailing. Fabric texture, stitching, curtain heading, rods, tracks, and finishing details work together to create the final result.

Guidance before production

Expert curtain consultancy for your interior

Our curtain consultancy service helps answer these questions before production begins. We assess the room and recommend suitable fabrics, colors, textures, curtain styles, and fitting options based on both the interior design and practical requirements of the space. This approach helps reduce guesswork and allows clients to make more confident decisions.

Choosing the right curtain starts with asking the right questions.

  • How much sunlight reaches the room?
  • Is a complete blackout required, or should some natural light remain?
  • Does the room need more privacy?
  • Should the curtain visually enlarge the space?
  • Which fabric works with the existing sofa, wall color, and flooring?
  • Would a single curtain layer work, or would a sheer-and-blackout combination be more suitable?

Our working process

A successful curtain project depends on good planning as much as good fabric.

Consultation

We begin by discussing your interior, preferences, functional requirements, budget considerations, and project expectations.
