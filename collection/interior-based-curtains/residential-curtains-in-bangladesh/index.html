By room and home style

Types of residential curtains by room and home style

Every room asks something different of its curtains. These are the options we most often plan for homes across Bangladesh.

01 / Residential

Bedroom curtains

Bedrooms need darkness and quiet. Blackout-lined, layered or soft velvet curtains keep out early sunlight and street glare, so sleep is deeper and rooms stay cooler.

02 / Residential

Living room curtains

This is the room guests see first. Sheer-and-drape layers, linen blends and jacquard weaves filter daylight gently and give the space a finished look.

03 / Residential

Dining room curtains

Semi-sheer and light-filtering fabrics keep a dining area bright without harsh glare, and they pair easily with table and chair finishes.

04 / Residential

Kitchen curtains

Short, lightweight and easy to wash is the rule here. Cotton and polyester blends cope with steam, oil and frequent laundering.

05 / Residential

Children's room curtains

Gentle colours, playful patterns and blackout backing help children nap and sleep on time, and the fabrics stand up to rough handling.

06 / Residential

Study room and home office curtains

Glare on a screen or page ruins concentration. Neutral linen, roller-style or light-filtering curtains give a calm, even workspace with privacy.

07 / Residential

Balcony door curtains

Full-length panels, ideally layered, control the afternoon sun and keep the room private while the balcony stays usable.

08 / Residential

Apartment curtains

City flats sit close to neighbours and traffic. Sheer-plus-blackout combinations give privacy at night and soft light by day.

09 / Residential

Duplex and villa curtains

Tall windows, stairwells and double-height living areas call for custom-measured, premium fabrics with consistent drape across floors.

10 / Residential

Luxury and smart-home curtains

Heavy velvets, designer weaves and motorised tracks suit homes that want a high-end finish with control at the touch of a button.

For a Bangladeshi home

How to choose residential curtains for a Bangladeshi home

Choosing the right curtains for a Bangladeshi home means balancing style with the realities of our climate: intense sunlight, heavy monsoon humidity, and dust from busy city streets. The best choice comes down to matching fabric, light control, and maintenance needs for each room so your curtains look good and last through the seasons.

  • Start with the room's job. Decide whether you need darkness, daylight, privacy, or heat control, then choose the fabric.
  • Pick fabrics that handle humidity and dust. Washable, color-stable materials keep their shape and shade after repeated cleaning.
  • Measure properly. Width, fullness, and floor clearance determine whether curtains look tailored or off-the-peg.
  • Coordinate with your interior. Tones, textures, and formality should echo your walls, flooring, and furniture. Our interior-based curtain service is built around this.
  • Plan for the seasons. Heavier, lined curtains suit winter and monsoon, while airy weaves suit summer. See our seasonal curtains .
  • Choose lasting styles over trends. Neutral colors and quality textures adapt when you redecorate.

Comfort, privacy and protection

Why the right residential curtains matter

The right curtains do more than dress a window: in Bangladesh’s strong sun and monsoon humidity, they control heat and glare, protect your furniture from fading, and give you privacy in closely built neighborhoods. A well-chosen fabric also resists mildew and wear, so your curtains stay fresh and functional through every season.

  • Light you can control: Adjust the room from bright and open to fully dark.
  • Privacy at home: Block outside views without losing daylight.
  • Cooler rooms: Lined fabrics cut solar heat, which can ease air-conditioning use.
  • Less dust and noise: Dense weaves soften street sound and catch airborne dust.
  • A complete interior: Well-fitted curtains make furniture, walls, and flooring look intentional.
