Winter curtains in Bangladesh | Curtain Canvas

Winter in Bangladesh is short, but it can feel sharp. Mornings and nights turn cold, dry air carries dust, and thin summer curtains do little to stop chilly drafts around windows and balcony doors. The right winter curtain changes how a room feels. It softens the cold edge of a window, keeps warmth inside, adds privacy for early dark evenings, and brings a richer, cosier look to the space.

Why winter calls for heavier curtains

A light curtain that works well in the heat does very little in cold weather. Winter curtains do several jobs at once:

  • Reduce drafts: Dense fabric and lining help cut cold air that creeps in around window frames and doors.
  • Hold in warmth: Heavier curtains act as a soft barrier between a warm room and a cold window.
  • Add privacy: Earlier sunsets mean lights come on sooner, and a fuller curtain keeps the room private.
  • Block dust: Dry winter air carries more dust, and tightly woven fabric helps keep it out of the room.
  • Set the mood: Rich textures and deeper tones make a room feel warm and settled.
  • Improve sleep: Thick curtains darken bedrooms and soften street noise on cold nights.

Types of winter curtains we offer

Curtain Canvas offers a variety of winter curtains to make your home feel warm, cosy, and inviting. Explore different fabrics, textures, and designs that suit your interior style and seasonal needs.

Velvet curtains

Velvet has a dense pile, a soft drape and a rich look. It suits living rooms, master bedrooms and formal spaces where you want warmth and presence.

Chenille curtains

Chenille has a plush, textured feel and a heavy hang. It adds warmth and depth to a room and works well in family lounges and bedrooms.

Jacquard curtains

Woven patterns give jacquard a refined, classic look. The fabric is substantial and works well when you want winter warmth with a decorative touch.

Thermal and interlined curtains

An extra layer inside the curtain adds insulation and body. These are useful for large windows, balcony doors and rooms that feel cold in the mornings and at night.

Blackout winter curtains

Dense blackout fabric darkens the room and blocks drafts. It suits bedrooms, nurseries and anyone who sleeps late on cold mornings.

Layered curtains

A heavy outer curtain with a light sheer behind it lets you choose between full warmth and soft daylight. On sunny winter afternoons you can open the heavy layer and let natural light in.

Dobby and woven textured curtains

Subtle woven texture adds weight and interest without feeling formal. A good choice for study rooms, dining areas and family spaces.

How to choose fabrics for winter

Choosing the right curtain fabric can make your rooms feel warmer and more comfortable during winter. Consider thicker materials, soft textures, and suitable linings to match your home’s needs and style.

  • Fabric weight: Heavier fabrics hold warmth better. Match weight to the size and exposure of the window.
  • Lining: A thermal or interlined layer improves insulation, especially for large or north-facing windows.
  • Weave and pile: A dense weave or plush pile cuts drafts more effectively than a loose weave.
  • Color: Deeper, warmer tones such as burgundy, olive, navy, chocolate, and charcoal feel cozy. Neutral creams and warm greys keep rooms bright.
  • Fullness: Generous pleating makes curtains look richer and covers the window better.
  • Length: Floor-length curtains block drafts at the bottom edge and look more complete.
  • Care: Check whether the fabric needs dry cleaning or can be washed, and plan for dust between seasons.

Practical tips for winter curtains

The right winter curtains can help create a cozy atmosphere and reduce cold air near your windows. Follow these simple tips to choose, hang, and care for curtains throughout the colder months.

  • Think about hardware early: Heavier curtains need strong rods or tracks and secure brackets.
  • Go full-length: A curtain that reaches the floor blocks drafts and looks more polished.
  • Add fullness: More fabric in the pleats gives better coverage and a richer look.
  • Layer for flexibility: Pair a heavy curtain with a sheer so you can let in sunlight on bright days.
  • Open them in the daytime: On sunny winter days, letting sunlight in warms the room naturally.
  • Clean before storing or switching: Dust builds up in dry weather, so refresh curtains before the season changes.
  • Choose timeless tones: Neutral and classic colors can stay up beyond winter with a lighter layer added in warm weather.
