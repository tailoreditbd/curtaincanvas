Structure before execution

The significance of curtain planning

Curtain projects that span multiple rooms, floors, or an entire new-build home run into problems when there's no plan behind them. A proper plan solves these problems before they start, not after.

  • Rooms get finished out of order, leaving some windows bare for months
  • Budgets run out partway through a project because spending wasn't mapped in advance
  • Fabric choices in one room clash with adjoining spaces because they weren't planned together
  • Delivery and installation dates clash with move-in timelines or events
  • Repeat orders are needed because early decisions weren't planned against the whole project

A complete property plan

Our curtain planning includes

Our curtain planning focuses on your room’s layout, window size, natural light, privacy needs, and interior style. We carefully plan fabrics, colors, layers, and curtain types to create a practical and elegant look that fits your space.

01

Whole-property mapping

We look at every room needing curtains together, not in isolation, so fabrics, colors, and styles flow logically from space to space.

02

Budget allocation

Your total budget is broken down room by room, so you know exactly what's being spent where and can adjust priorities early.

03

Timeline and sequencing

We build an order of operations—which rooms are measured, ordered, and installed first—based on your move-in date, renovation schedule, or event deadline.

04

Supplier and production scheduling

Fabric lead times, stitching turnaround, and installation slots are planned in advance to avoid last-minute delays.

05

Documentation

You receive a clear, written plan covering measurements, fabric choices, costs, and dates for every room, so nothing is left to memory.

From overview to execution

How the curtain planning is done

Our curtain planning starts by assessing your windows, room layout, lighting, privacy needs, and interior style. Based on these factors, we recommend suitable curtain types, fabrics, colors, and installation options for your space. Here is our planning process:

1. Project overview

Share the full scope of your project: rooms, timeline, and budget.

2. Room-by-room review

We assess each space and note fabric, style, and measurement needs.

3. Plan drafting

A complete plan is put together, covering sequencing, budget, and delivery dates.

4. Client sign-off

You review and approve the plan before any orders are placed.

5. Execution and tracking

Curtain Canvas manages sourcing, stitching, and installation against the agreed plan.
