Plan before the fabric is cut

Why curtain consultancy matters

Curtains are a long-term investment in a room. A mismatched fabric weight, the wrong pleat style, or curtains hung at the wrong height can undercut an otherwise well-designed interior. Our consultancy service is built to catch the below issues before the fabric is cut, not after. Common problems we help clients avoid include:

  • Fabric that lets in too much or too little light for the room's purpose
  • Colors and patterns that clash with existing furnishings
  • Incorrect curtain length or width, leaving rooms looking unfinished
  • Poor track or rod selection for the window type and ceiling height
  • Overspending on fabric that won't perform well in Bangladesh's humidity and sunlight

What our curtain consultancy includes

Our curtain consultancy takes the guesswork out of window treatments, covering everything from fabric choice to final measurements. Here's what's included when you book a session with Curtain Canvas.

01

Space and light assessment

We look at how each room is used, how much natural light it receives through the day, and what level of privacy or blackout is needed, then recommend fabric weights and linings accordingly.

02

Fabric and material guidance

From breathable cottons and linens suited to Bangladesh's climate to heavier blackout and thermal fabrics for bedrooms and media rooms, we match material to purpose and budget.

03

Style and design direction

Pleat styles, curtain lengths, layering with sheers, color palettes, and how curtains should relate to existing wall colors, flooring, and furniture, all covered in the consultation.

04

Measurement and technical planning

Accurate width and drop measurements, track and rod recommendations, and guidance on mounting height and width for the most flattering proportions.

05

Budget planning

A clear breakdown of where your budget is best spent—fabric, lining, hardware, or tailoring—so there are no surprises later.

How the consultancy process works

Getting started with Curtain Canvas is simple and structured, from your first conversation to the final installation. Here's how the process unfolds, step by step.

1. Initial consultation: Share your space, requirements, and style preferences with our team, in person or remotely.

2. On-site or virtual assessment: We evaluate the windows, light conditions, and room layout.

3. Recommendations: You receive fabric samples, style options, and a tailored plan suited to your rooms and budget.

4. Measurement and quotation: Precise measurements are taken, and a detailed quotation is prepared.

5. Execution: Once you approve the plan, Curtain Canvas handles sourcing, stitching, and installation.
