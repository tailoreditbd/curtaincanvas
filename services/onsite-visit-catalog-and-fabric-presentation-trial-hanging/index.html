See the real look before you order

Onsite visit, catalog & fabric presentation + trial hanging in Bangladesh

Photos and swatch cards only tell part of the story. Fabric that looks rich on a screen can look completely different once it's hanging in front of your own windows, under your own lighting, or against your own wall color. That's the gap this service is built to close.

With Curtain Canvas's onsite visit service, our team comes directly to your home, office, or project site anywhere in Bangladesh. We bring the full catalog, real fabric swatches, and—where useful—sample panels we can hang temporarily on your windows so you can judge drape, light control, and color match in the actual room before committing to an order.

Book an Onsite Visit

Confidence in your own space

Why an on-site visit matters

An onsite visit helps us understand your windows, room layout, lighting, and interior style before recommending a curtain solution. It allows us to take accurate measurements and suggest fabrics, fullness, hardware, and finishing that work naturally with your space.

  • Accurate color matching. Screens and printed swatch cards shift color. Seeing fabric under your own lighting removes the guesswork.
  • Real fit for real windows. Curtains are judged properly only when seen at scale, against the actual window dimensions and ceiling height.
  • Fewer costly mistakes. Choosing fabric without a trial hang is one of the most common reasons customers end up unhappy with ready-made or blindly ordered curtains.
  • Convenience. You don't need to visit a showroom. The consultation, catalog, and samples come to you, wherever you are in Bangladesh.
  • Confident decisions. By the end of the visit, you know exactly what your curtains will look like—not just what they might look like.

A complete in-room presentation

What happens during the visit

Our representative doesn't just drop off a catalog and leave. Each visit is structured to give you everything you need to make a confident decision.

01

Space and window assessment

We walk through the room with you, look at window shapes and sizes, note the natural light direction, and take in the existing interior style—furniture, wall color, flooring—so every fabric suggestion actually fits the space.

02

Catalog walkthrough

We bring our full curtain catalog on-site, covering residential, commercial, and hospitality ranges. Rather than browsing on a phone screen, you go through printed pages and physical fabric books at your own pace, with our consultant explaining pleat styles, lining options, and finishing choices along the way.

03

Fabric presentation

Selected fabric swatches are laid out against your walls, furniture, and existing decor. We explain texture, weight, light-filtering behavior, and durability for each option and narrow the choices down to what genuinely suits your room and your budget.

04

Trial hanging

For your shortlisted fabrics, we can hang sample lengths temporarily on the actual window. This lets you see how the fabric falls, how it filters sunlight during the day, and how the color reads against your wall—something no catalog photo can fully show.

05

Notes for your final order

Everything discussed during the visit—preferred fabric, color, pleat style, and any measurement notes—is recorded and carried forward into the curtain planning and customization stage, so nothing gets lost between the visit and the final curtain.
