When a customized curtain solution makes sense

Ready-made curtains suit simple windows and tight deadlines. Customization earns its place when the room asks for more, for example, when:

  • The window is unusually wide, tall or shaped, such as full-height glazing, corner windows, or a run of connected panels
  • You want a specific look, from a particular pleat to a layered sheer-and-drape combination
  • A room needs dependable light control, like a bedroom that must go fully dark or an office that needs glare relief without losing daylight
  • You are furnishing several rooms or floors and want every set to match
  • You plan to use a heavier fabric or a motorized track that needs properly matched hardware

What we customize in every curtain

Every customized curtain is planned in five layers, so each choice supports the others.

1. Fabric and lining

Choose from sheer, linen, velvet, blackout, and blended fabrics, then add the right lining. Lining gives the curtain body, controls light and heat, and protects the face fabric from sun fading.

2. Dimensions and fullness

We cut to your exact width and drop and agree on the hem length with you, from a clean floor-skimming finish to a soft break at the floor. We also set how generously the fabric is gathered: fuller for deep, rich folds, lighter for a cleaner line.

3. Header and pleat style

Pinch pleat, wave, pencil pleat, or eyelet. The header decides how formal the curtain looks and how smoothly it moves along the track or rod.

4. Hardware and operation

Rods, tracks, brackets, and finials are matched to the weight of the fabric and the width of the window. Where daily convenience matters, motorized tracks with remote or app control can be added.

5. Finishing details

Borders, contrast trims, tiebacks, and weighted hems complete the curtain and help it hang straight.

Customized curtains for every kind of space

From family homes to busy offices and hotel lobbies, every space asks something different of its windows. Our customized curtain solution adapts the fabric, lining, and hardware to how each room is actually used.

For homes

Bedrooms can pair blackout drapes with a sheer layer for day and night flexibility. Living rooms can carry heavier, more decorative drapes, and home theaters benefit from light-tight lining and full-width coverage. We can keep one design language across the whole house while adjusting fabric and lining room by room.

For offices and workspaces

Offices need calm, professional windows: even coverage, glare control for screens, and colors that agree with your brand and furnishings. We produce matching sets across every window and floor, so the whole workplace looks deliberate.

For hotels and restaurants

Hospitality interiors need curtains that look good, handle daily use, and repeat consistently across many rooms. We specify fabrics and hardware for durability, plan quantities and finishes as one coordinated order, and adjust privacy levels for guest rooms, dining areas, and lobbies.

Built for Bangladesh's heat, humidity, and sunlight

A curtain that looks perfect in a showroom can struggle in a sun-facing room during a monsoon. We factor local conditions into every recommendation:

  • Strong sun: Fade-resistant fabrics and proper lining keep colors true for longer.
  • Humidity: For damp-prone rooms, we steer you toward fabrics that dry quickly and keep their shape.
  • Dust and upkeep: We explain how each fabric should be cleaned, so you can choose a low-maintenance option for busy rooms.
  • Heat and glare: Lined, layered curtains soften harsh afternoon light and take some of the heat out of it.

How our customized curtain service works

Most customized orders are ready within a few weeks of design approval, depending on fabric availability and the number of windows. Here is our process:

1. Share your brief.

Tell us about the room, how you use it, and the look you want. Photos and reference images help. If you are still undecided, start with our curtain consultancy .

2. We measure on site.

Our team measures every window and notes ceiling height, wall space on each side, and anything that could obstruct the curtain.

3. See it before you commit.

We present fabric options and, where useful, arrange a trial hanging so you can judge color and drape in your own light.

4. Approve the specification.

You review the final fabric, lining, pleat, hardware, and quotation. Nothing is cut until you sign off.

5. Tailoring.

Your curtains are cut, stitched, and finished to the approved specification.

6. Installation and final check.

We hang the curtains, adjust the fall, test the operation and show you how to care for them.
