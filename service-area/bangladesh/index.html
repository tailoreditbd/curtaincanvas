Our luxury curtain services in Bangladesh

Choosing beautiful fabric is only one part of creating the perfect curtain. Incorrect measurements, unsuitable materials, weak stitching, and poor installation can leave curtains looking uneven, controlling too little sunlight, or failing to match the room’s interior. Ready-made solutions also rarely provide the right balance of privacy, comfort, elegance, and functionality for every window.

Complete support

Luxury and premium curtain services, at Curtain Canvas

Our service is designed to make buying custom curtains simple, convenient, and reliable. Whether you are decorating a home, apartment, office, hotel, restaurant, or commercial space, Curtain Canvas manages every stage with careful attention to design and functionality.

01

Free consultation

Selecting the right curtain can be confusing when there are many fabrics, colours, designs, lining options, and hanging styles available. Our free curtain consultation helps you understand which solution will work best for your space.

02

Free catalogue delivery

Curtain Canvas makes fabric and design selection more convenient by delivering a curtain catalogue to your doorstep. You can explore suitable colours, textures, patterns, and curtain styles from the comfort of your home or office.

03

Laser Measurement and Installation

Accurate measurement is essential for achieving balanced curtain length, proper fabric coverage, smooth movement, and a polished finish. Our team uses laser-assisted measurement tools to record window dimensions and plan each curtain according to the space.

04

Five-Year After-Sales Service

Our responsibility does not end immediately after curtain installation. Curtain Canvas provides five years of after-sales service to support customers with applicable adjustments, installation-related assistance, and guidance on maintaining their curtain system.
