What makes a curtain "high-quality"?

Anyone can call a premium curtain. These are the details that actually separate a well-made curtain from an ordinary one and the ones we pay attention to on every Banani project:

  • Fabric with substance: A good fabric hangs evenly, resists creasing, and holds its color in strong daylight.
  • Considered lining: The right lining controls light, adds body, and protects the face fabric from sun exposure.
  • Clean, precise stitching: Straight seams, even hems, and matched pattern repeats make the difference between "bought" and "tailored."
  • Correct proportions: Width and length are set to your window and ceiling height, so the curtain falls naturally instead of looking short or skimpy.
  • Reliable hardware: Rods and tracks should carry the weight of the curtain and glide smoothly for years.
  • Neat installation: Level fitting, tidy brackets, and final adjustments on site complete the finish.

Why made-to-measure works better in Banani

Window sizes in Dhaka apartments and commercial units vary widely, from tall living-room glazing to compact bedroom windows and shopfront panes. Off-the-shelf curtains are cut to standard sizes, which often means the fabric is too short, too narrow, or awkward at the corners.

A made-to-measure curtain removes that guesswork:

  • Exact sizing: Height and width are taken from your actual window, not an approximation.
  • Fabric chosen for the job: You decide the balance of privacy, light control, texture, and color.
  • Style tailored to the room: Pleat type, layering, and overall drape are selected to match the character of the space.
  • Matching hardware: Rods, tracks, and accessories are chosen to suit the curtain's weight and the wall or ceiling it is fixed to.
