A neighbourhood of character

Curtains for the luxury home of Wari

Wari is one of Dhaka's historic residential neighborhoods and is widely recognized as the city's first planned residential area. Its mix of heritage and modern urban living makes interior choices particularly important for homeowners who want to preserve character while adding contemporary comfort.

At Curtain Canvas, curtain planning for a Wari property can consider:

  • Historic residential identity: Wari developed as one of Dhaka's earliest planned residential neighborhoods, giving the area a character different from many newer parts of the city.
  • Heritage and modern homes: Older houses now stand alongside renovated properties and newer buildings, creating opportunities for both classic and contemporary curtain styles.
  • Part of Old Dhaka: Wari combines access to the cultural character of Old Dhaka with established residential surroundings.
  • Distinctive local landmarks: The area includes notable historic places such as the Christian Cemetery and Baldha Garden, adding to Wari's long-standing identity.
  • Interior needs vary from home to home: Different window sizes, room layouts, sunlight conditions, privacy requirements, and architectural styles make custom curtain planning especially useful for Wari properties.
