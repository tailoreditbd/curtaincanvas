Curtains for the refined lifestyle of Gulshan 2

Life in Gulshan 2 moves between apartments, boardrooms, cafés, and hotel lobbies, and each setting asks something different from a curtain. A living room wants softness and elegance. A bedroom wants darkness and quiet. An office wants a crisp, professional line. A restaurant wants atmosphere.

Our planning looks at the details that matter most in this neighborhood:

  • Window scale: Tall, wide, or floor-to-ceiling windows need the right fullness and fall to look balanced rather than bare.
  • Sun and glare: Sheer, blackout, and layered combinations help manage strong daylight and warm rooms.
  • Street-side privacy: Windows facing busy roads or neighboring buildings can be covered without losing brightness.
  • Sound comfort: Heavier, lined fabrics help soften outside noise in bedrooms and meeting rooms.
  • Color and texture: Fabrics are chosen to sit comfortably with your furniture, flooring, walls, and lighting.
  • Finish quality: Clean stitching, even hems, and well-placed tracks keep the final look polished.

Custom curtain design service

Two windows of the same size can call for very different curtains once the room around them changes. Our custom curtain design service in Gulshan 2 shapes each solution around your window and your interior, rather than trimming a ready-made option to fit.

  • Made-to-measure sizing: Panels are cut from the true height and width of your window.
  • Fabric of your choice: Select materials by color, texture, privacy, and light-control needs.
  • Custom heading and fullness: Pleats, folds, and layers are set to match the character of the room.
  • Lining options: The right lining improves privacy, blocks light, and gives the curtain a fuller hang.
  • Matched hardware: Rods, tracks, and accessories are picked to complement both the curtain and the room.
