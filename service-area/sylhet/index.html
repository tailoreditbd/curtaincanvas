Why curtains in Sylhet need a considered choice

Sylhet's setting shapes what a curtain has to do. A fabric that suits a dry, sunny city can struggle here, so every recommendation we make starts from local conditions.

  • Rain and humidity.  Sylhet is among the wettest parts of Bangladesh. Fabrics, linings, and hems should hold their shape in damp air, and curtains should hang clear of sills where rain can blow in.
  • Changing light.  Hard summer sun gives way to soft grey light in the monsoon. One layer rarely handles both, so a sheer panel paired with a heavier lined curtain gives you control through the day.
  • Views and privacy.  River-facing flats, hillside homes, and closely built apartment blocks each need a different answer on privacy. We match the curtain to what your window actually looks like.
  • Homes that are not always occupied.  Many Sylhet families have close ties overseas, and some homes stand quiet for months. Lined curtains help protect furnishings from sun fading, and we can plan the project by call and WhatsApp before you travel.
  • Visitors and guests.  Guesthouses, restaurants, and resorts serve travelers heading to Jaflong, Ratargul, and the Shahjalal Dargah. Their curtains need to look polished and cope with constant use.

From first call to final fold: our custom curtain process

Made-to-measure curtains fit better, fall better, and last longer than standard sizes. This is how a Sylhet project runs from start to finish.

  1. Consultation.  We learn how each room is used, the style you prefer, and what the curtains must do.
  2. Measurement.  Every window and fitting area is measured, so width, drop, and rod or track position are right.
  3. Fabric and design.  With our guidance, you choose fabric, color, pleat style, lining, and finishing details.
  4. Tailoring.  The curtains are cut, stitched, and finished to the approved measurements.
  5. Installation and inspection.  Our team fits the hardware, hangs the curtains, and checks alignment, movement, and fall beforehand.
