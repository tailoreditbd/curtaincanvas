Window treatments that suit life around Mohakhali

Because the area blends living and working spaces, we begin every project with a simple question: what does this room need to do every day? Depending on the answer, we weigh factors such as

  • Glare and heat: Strong sunlight can make screens hard to read and rooms uncomfortable. Lined or layered curtains help you control it.
  • Privacy: Windows that face neighboring buildings or busy roads often need more coverage than a single sheer can offer.
  • A calmer atmosphere: Fuller, heavier fabrics add softness to a room and help it feel more settled.
  • Ease of upkeep: Busy locations collect dust, so fabric choice and cleaning habits matter, especially for commercial spaces.
  • Interior style: Color, texture, and drape should sit comfortably with your furniture, walls, and floors.
  • Long-term durability: Hardware and stitching should handle regular daily use without sagging or fraying.

Why made-to-measure beats ready-made

Ready-made curtains come in fixed sizes. In real buildings, windows rarely follow those sizes, and the result is curtains that stop short of the floor, leave gaps at the sides, or bunch awkwardly.

With made-to-measure work from Curtain Canvas:

  • Every dimension is taken from your window, so the fall is even and the proportions look deliberate.
  • You choose the fabric and lining to balance privacy, light control, and feel.
  • Style details are yours to set, from pleats and layering to finishing touches.
  • Hardware is matched to the weight of the curtain and the surface it mounts on.
