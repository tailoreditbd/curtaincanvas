Dhaka’s growing demand for premium and luxury interiors

Dhaka is Bangladesh’s leading center for business, modern housing, architecture, and interior design. The 2022 Population and Housing Census recorded more than 14.73 million people in Dhaka District, while Bangladesh’s total urban population exceeded 52 million.

Rapid urban growth is increasing the demand for well-planned apartments, offices, hotels, restaurants, and commercial interiors. Bangladesh’s urban population grew by approximately 11.5 million between 2011 and 2022, reflecting the continuing expansion of city living.

  • Luxury apartments are expanding across Gulshan, Banani, Baridhara, Bashundhara, Uttara, Dhanmondi, Mirpur, and other parts of Dhaka.
  • Large windows and glass-fronted buildings require effective sunlight, glare, and privacy control.
  • Premium homeowners increasingly prefer curtains designed around furniture, wall colors, lighting, and room proportions.
  • Offices, hotels, restaurants, and clinics need practical curtain solutions that also support their brand image.
  • Dhaka’s heat, humidity, dust, and intense sunlight make fabric quality and professional installation especially important.
