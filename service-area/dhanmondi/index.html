Planned around your space

Curtains for the premium lifestyle of Dhanmondi

Dhanmondi has a mix of residential and commercial properties, which means curtain requirements can vary significantly from one space to another. A family living room may need an elegant layered curtain that keeps the room bright during the day, while a bedroom may require stronger privacy and light control. Offices often need a cleaner, more professional finish, while restaurants and hospitality spaces depend heavily on atmosphere.

At Curtain Canvas, curtain planning can consider:

  • Interior style: Curtain color, texture, and design should complement furniture, walls, flooring, and décor.
  • Natural light: Sheer, blackout, or layered curtains can be selected according to the amount of daylight entering the room.
  • Privacy: Bedrooms and street-facing windows may need greater visual privacy.
  • Room proportion: Proper curtain height and width can make windows appear more balanced and interiors more refined.
  • Daily convenience: Curtain systems and fabrics should suit how frequently the window is used.
  • Overall ambience: The right fabric and fall can make a living room feel warmer, a bedroom more relaxing, or an office more polished.
